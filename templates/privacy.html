Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and the expense data you enter (amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions). We may also collect basic usage data such as login timestamps to maintain your account security.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expenses, generating spending summaries, and authenticating your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, to protect your personal and financial information. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may use third-party services for hosting and infrastructure, and these providers are bound by their own privacy policies and data protection obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We will respond to your inquiry as promptly as possible.
